Draft for the owner's review. These terms were written in plain language without a lawyer.

1. What Recipes does

Recipes keeps your recipes and pictures, syncs them between your devices, and can turn a web page, a photo or a text into a recipe draft with an AI model. You need an Apple or Google account to sign in and must be at least 16 years old.

2. Free use and Recipes Pro

Free use includes sync, 50 MB of pictures and one AI import. Recipes Pro is a subscription (monthly or yearly) bought in the App Store or on Google Play. It includes 30 AI imports a month, 500 MB of pictures, translations and sharing by link. The price shown in the store applies. The subscription renews until you cancel it. You cancel it in the store where you bought it (App Store: Settings, your name, Subscriptions; Google Play: Payments and subscriptions), not in the app. Deleting your account does not cancel a store subscription. Refunds follow the store's rules.

3. AI results

AI drafts are made by a general-purpose model. They can be wrong or incomplete, for example in quantities, times or allergens. Check every draft before you save or cook from it. Nothing is saved until you confirm it. You can report a wrong or offensive result in the app.

4. Acceptable use

Do not upload or share content that is illegal, that infringes other people's rights, or that is abusive. Do not try to get around the limits, to use the service for others in bulk, or to attack it. You are responsible for what you store and share. Shared recipes can be reported; a report ends the link.

5. Suspension

We may suspend an account for reported illegal or abusive content, or for abuse of the quotas or of payments. A suspended account can still read its recipes and pictures, export a backup, sign out and delete itself; everything else stops. The app shows the reason. A store subscription keeps billing during a suspension; cancel it in the store if you wish. To appeal, write to: OWNER-LEGAL-PLACEHOLDER (appeal contact, filled in by the owner).

6. Ending

You can delete your account at any time in the app's settings or on the web page "Delete your account". The deletion is final.

Your statutory rights as a consumer are not affected.

We tell you in the app before changes to these terms take effect. German law applies; mandatory consumer protection rules of the country where you live stay in force.
