Tomato Basil Pasta

Ingredients
400 g spaghetti
500 g cherry tomatoes
3 cloves garlic
4 tbsp olive oil
1 bunch basil
50 g parmesan

Method
1. Boil the pasta in salted water.
2. Fry sliced garlic in the oil, add halved tomatoes and cook for 8 minutes.
3. Toss the drained pasta with the sauce, torn basil and grated parmesan.
