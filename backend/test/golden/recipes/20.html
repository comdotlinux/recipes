Miso Ramen

Serves 2

Ingredients

  • 2 portions ramen noodles
  • 4 cups chicken stock
  • 3 tbsp white miso
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 soft-boiled eggs
  • 2 spring onions
  • 1 cup corn

Method

  1. Heat the stock and whisk in miso and soy sauce without boiling.
  2. Cook the noodles separately and drain.
  3. Divide noodles into bowls, pour over the broth and top with eggs, spring onions and corn.
