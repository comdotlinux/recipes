Banana Bread

Serves 10

Ingredients

  • 3 ripe bananas
  • 1/3 cup melted butter
  • 3/4 cup brown sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 1 1/2 cups flour

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 350°F and grease a loaf pan.
  2. Mash the bananas and mix in butter, sugar, egg and vanilla.
  3. Stir in baking soda and flour.
  4. Bake for 60 minutes until a skewer comes out clean.
