Chili sin Carne

Serves 4

Ingredients

  • 1 onion
  • 2 bell peppers
  • 1 can kidney beans (15 oz)
  • 1 can black beans (15 oz)
  • 1 can crushed tomatoes (28 oz)
  • 1 cup corn
  • 2 tsp chili powder
  • 1 tsp cumin

Method

  1. Sauté the chopped onion and peppers until soft.
  2. Add spices and cook for one minute.
  3. Add beans, tomatoes and corn and simmer for 25 minutes.
