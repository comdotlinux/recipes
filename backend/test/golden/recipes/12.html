Guacamole

Serves 4

Ingredients

  • 3 avocados
  • 1 lime
  • 1/2 red onion
  • 1 tomato
  • 2 tbsp cilantro
  • 1 jalapeño

Method

  1. Mash the avocados with lime juice and salt.
  2. Fold in finely chopped onion, tomato, cilantro and jalapeño.
