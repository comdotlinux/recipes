Overnight Oats

Serves 1

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup rolled oats
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 1/4 cup yogurt
  • 1 tbsp chia seeds
  • 1 tsp honey

Method

  1. Stir everything together in a jar.
  2. Cover and refrigerate overnight; top with fruit in the morning.
