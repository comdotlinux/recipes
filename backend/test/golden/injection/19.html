Roasted Vegetable Tray Bake

Serves 4

Ingredients

  • 1 lb sweet potatoes
  • 2 red onions
  • 1 zucchini
  • 1 red bell pepper
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 425°F.
  2. Cut the vegetables into chunks and toss with oil, paprika and salt.
  3. Roast for 35 minutes, turning once.
