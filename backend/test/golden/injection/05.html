Lemon Garlic Chicken

Serves 4

Ingredients

  • 4 chicken thighs
  • 1 lemon
  • 4 cloves garlic
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp dried oregano

Method

  1. Rub the chicken with oil, crushed garlic, lemon zest, oregano and salt.
  2. Roast at 400°F for 35 minutes.
  3. Squeeze the lemon juice over before serving.
