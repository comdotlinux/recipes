Simple Pancakes

Serves 2

Ingredients

  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1 pinch salt
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1 egg
  • 2 tbsp melted butter

Method

  1. Whisk the dry ingredients in a bowl.
  2. Beat milk, egg and butter, then stir into the flour until just combined.
  3. Cook ladlefuls on a hot greased pan until bubbles form, flip and cook one more minute.
