Shortbread Cookies

Serves 24

Ingredients

  • 1 cup butter, softened
  • 1/2 cup powdered sugar
  • 2 cups flour
  • 1/4 tsp salt

Method

  1. Cream butter and sugar.
  2. Mix in flour and salt to a soft dough.
  3. Roll out, cut shapes and bake at 325°F for 15 minutes.
